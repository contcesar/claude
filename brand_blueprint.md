## Niche
Journaling for business clarity, decision-making, and personal performance

## Target Audience
- Business owners, entrepreneurs, and agency owners
- Adults aged 30–50
- High achievers who feel mentally scattered or overwhelmed
- People who want to think more clearly and make better decisions
- Professionals who are skeptical of "woo-woo" journaling — they want practical ROI

## Brand Voice
- Direct and honest, not fluffy or motivational-poster-ish
- Conversational but credible — like advice from a trusted peer, not a life coach
- Uses specific examples and real outcomes over vague promises
- No jargon, no buzzwords
- Focuses on business outcomes: clarity, fewer bad decisions, less stress, more focus
- Occasionally vulnerable — willing to share what wasn't working

## Content Pillars
1. Journaling for decision-making (how to use journaling to make faster, better calls)
2. Morning and evening routines for founders and operators
3. Mental clarity and focus systems (reducing cognitive load)
4. Journaling methods explained practically (brain dump, gratitude, prompted, free-write)
5. Productivity and performance for busy professionals

## Phrases to Avoid
- "Game changer"
- "Dive in" / "Let's dive into"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Leverage" (unless used naturally)
- "Unleash"
- Em dashes used for emphasis (—)
- Bullet lists that start with random emojis

## What Success Looks Like for Your Audience
Someone watches your video and walks away with one concrete thing they can do in the next 24 hours that makes their business life feel less chaotic.
